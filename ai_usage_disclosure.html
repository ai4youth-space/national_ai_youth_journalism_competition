AI Usage Disclosure and Notes

This page explains how AI tools were used while researching, drafting, revising, and presenting the article, and what parts remained human-directed.

Author Responsibility

The article was written and edited under human direction by Jacqueline Liu. AI tools were used as support tools, not as a replacement for the author's judgment, reporting choices, or final editorial responsibility.

How AI Was Used

  • To help organize research notes and compare claims from multiple sources.
  • To assist with article structure, headline/dek options, and revision planning.

  • The decision to focus on the gap between technical correctness and musical feeling.
  • The interpretation of the author's own music-making experiments.
  • The final selection of wording, sources, layout, and presentation.

Verification Notes
